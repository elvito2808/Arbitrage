## Fee semantics and source binding

The pinned Hiero relay converts the mirror’s EthereumTransaction gas schedule from tinybars to weibars. Its public `eth_gasPrice` applies the configured price buffer. The simulator multiplies route gas units by the public RPC price and rounds up once to tinybars. Admission uses the configured price cap at or below twice the public price. This covers modeled execution gas and does not represent a network fee receipt. The relay passes estimation to a mirror contract-call simulation, with the estimate flag set. The examined relay version does not forward the supplied block parameter to that estimate. The simulator requests latest state and rejects successful-looking values if the public head observed before and after the estimation changes. The relay default 15 million transaction gas limit bounds accepted estimates.
