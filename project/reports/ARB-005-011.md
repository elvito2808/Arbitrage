## ARB-010 — Gas and 24-hour feasibility

Hashio `eth_gasPrice` returned 1,630,000,000,000 weibars per gas in 111 ms. Hedera's public mirror network fees endpoint returned 163 tinybars per gas for ContractCall and EthereumTransaction in 117 ms. The units agree since one tinybar is 10^10 weibars. A public POST to `/api/v1/contracts/call` with `estimate:true`, RouterV3 as `to` and the exact-output quote calldata returned 34,133 gas in 138 ms. This measures the view computation, not token movement.

For a swap cost model without wallet or key, sample historical successful same-direction transactions that match the intended router, swap entry point, route and account requirements. Use public pair Swap logs to locate transactions and mirror contract results or RPC receipts to obtain gas used. Multiply a conservative gas estimate by the current gas price, and use the receipt's effective gas price or mirror charged-fee records when checking historical cost. Keep transaction-type fees and any setup operations separate. To estimate a specific swap path directly, use unsigned `eth_estimateGas` or mirror simulation against a representative public state with an eligible sender address, calldata and value. Balance, allowance and association still affect EVM simulation, so an arbitrary zero-state sender can fail. The assignment used view calldata for the direct estimate.

One existing successful transaction emitting a Swap for the target pair used 244,829 gas. Its root contract was `0.0.10966181`. At the observed gas price this is an illustrative 0.39907127 HBAR gas component, or 0.79814254 HBAR at the configured 2× price cap. This one transaction does not establish either direction's expected gas or total fee. No signed transaction is needed for any of these reads. [Hedera Mirror Node OpenAPI](https://mainnet-public.mirrornode.hedera.com/api/v1/docs/openapi.yml)
